# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is a portfolio decision-maker reviewing historical investment
episodes, sell discipline, benchmark-relative performance, and repeatable risk
signals. The product is used as a focused research and decision-support tool,
not as a public marketing surface.

## Product Purpose

The platform turns portfolio transactions and market history into explainable
evidence about ownership decisions. Success means the user can distinguish
repeatable decision rules from outcomes dominated by a few exceptional stocks,
understand assumptions, and act on current holdings with appropriate caution.

## Brand Personality

Calm, rigorous, and candid. The interface should communicate analytical
confidence without overstating provisional or counterfactual results.

## Anti-references

Avoid trading-terminal clutter, gamified investing interfaces, decorative
finance imagery, exaggerated performance claims, and dashboards that use color
without labels to imply whether a result is good or bad.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the decision, then expose the evidence and assumptions.
2. Separate observed results from counterfactual or provisional calculations.
3. Make outlier sensitivity visible before presenting a strategy conclusion.
4. Use consistent units, dates, signs, and benchmark windows.
5. Preserve enough detail for every aggregate number to be audited.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, readable
contrast, responsive layouts, and redundant labels so meaning never depends on
color alone.
